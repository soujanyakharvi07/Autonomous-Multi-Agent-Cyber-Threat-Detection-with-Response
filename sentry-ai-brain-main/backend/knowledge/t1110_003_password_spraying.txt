T1110.003 Password Spraying (MITRE ATT&CK). Prototype summary: one or a few common passwords are tried against many different accounts so that per-account lockout limits are not reached. In logs this shows as failed logins from one source across many distinct usernames.

Detection: correlate failures per source address across accounts. Mitigations: multi-factor authentication, banned-password lists, throttling per source, alerting on many accounts failing from one origin.
